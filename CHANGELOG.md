# Changelog

All notable changes to **BoomListener** are recorded here, by version. This file is also the text of each GitHub Release, so it is written in English, for people who use the app.

Format inspired by [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/). Versioning: [SemVer](https://semver.org/) — `MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH`.

## [Unreleased]

### Changed
- **Heart in the full-screen player:** when a song is a favorite, the filled heart now takes the color of the cover, like the other buttons (when **Settings → Appearance** has the buttons-in-cover-color option on).

## [0.57.0] - 2026-11-19

### Added
- **Year-end Wrapped notification:** on December 1 (or any day in December you open the phone, once a year) the app can tell you your Wrapped is ready, and tapping it opens it. It is on by default but only sent if notifications are already allowed (the app never asks for permission because of it); without permission, a "Your Wrapped is ready" card appears on your Profile instead. You can turn it off in Settings → System → Notifications.
- **Wrapped:** on the Statistics page, a new "Wrapped" card (opens after 30 days of listening; before that it shows how many days are left) turns the period you picked into story-style cards: your top artists, the song you played the most (it plays right there while you watch, and your player is paused and restored when you leave), favorite genres, total time with your record day and streak, your favorite time of day, new artists you discovered, how your Top 5 moved month by month, and your top songs with a button to create a playlist with your Top 100 (that playlist is locked: it cannot be renamed or get new songs, so it stays a true Wrapped; you can still play, remove songs from or delete it). The Wrapped ends with a summary card (top artists, most played and total time) that also has its own Share button. Tap the right side to go forward, the left to go back, hold to pause (the bars and buttons fade away while you hold and fade back when you let go). A speaker button at the top silences the Wrapped song (and brings the sound back). The share button at the top always sends your Wrapped summary (the last card) as one image, from any card (the picture carries the BoomListener name and never any audio). Everything is calculated on your device.
- **Favorite time of day (groundwork):** the daily summary now also keeps how long you listened in each hour of the day, to power the Wrapped. Months from before this update have no hour information.
- **New Statistics page:** tapping the orange "This month" card on your Profile opens a full page, with the period you pick (4 weeks, this month, this year or all time): total time listened, plays, artists, genres and days, your record day, your longest streak and the day with the most different artists, and the Top 5 of artists, songs, albums and genres, each one opening a Top 100 with play counts. Artists show an arrow for how much they went up or down compared with the previous period (previous month, year or 4 weeks), and for the year there is a month-by-month view of how your Top 5 artists changed. Everything is calculated on your phone.
- **Select several genres, artists, playlists or albums in Library:** hold one to open its panel and tap "Select" (tapping the picture still just opens it). Then tap others to mark them, or use "select all", and shuffle or play the songs of everything you marked (a radio of those artists or genres), or add them to a playlist. A song that is in two marked groups plays once.
- **Total listening time on Home:** under the song count you now see how long your whole library lasts, in exact hours and minutes, e.g. "176 songs" and "10 h 24 min" (just minutes when it is under an hour).
- **A daily listening summary that is never erased (groundwork for the new statistics):** the app now keeps, per day, which songs, artists, albums and genres you listened to and for how long you really listened (including songs you skipped), so your statistics can reach back beyond the 1 year / 5,000 plays of the history. Your existing history and most-played counts are brought in automatically the first time (time listened before this version is estimated from the song length). It stays on your phone and is part of your backup. Nothing new is shown yet.
- **Songs with more than one artist now show all their photos:** where a name like "Delinquent, Kcat, Mike Delinquent Project" used to show a single photo or none, Artists (list and grid), Home and the "Your artists" row in Profile now fade quickly from one artist's photo to the next, every couple of seconds. An artist without a photo (including those for which Deezer only has its generic silhouette, which now also shows as the initial letter at the top of the artist page) now shows the cover of their newest release (the same one their artist page uses in the circle; if Deezer has nothing, the latest release on your device) instead of an empty person icon, and it takes part in the fade too. The "Artists of this song" panel does the same.
- **Animated icon in About:** the app icon now builds itself like a sonic boom each time you open About, and again every time you tap it. A speck flies in from the right and stops in silence; then the sound barrier breaks behind it, with a tremor, a flash, echoes, and the two arcs bursting out. If your phone's animations are turned off, the icon just shows up ready.
- **Open audio files with BoomListener:** BoomListener now shows up in "Open with" when you tap an audio file in a file manager or another app. A small player slides up over that app, with the cover (read from inside the file), title, progress bar and play/pause. It has its own player: it doesn't touch the queue or the song you were playing inside BoomListener (that one pauses while the file plays and picks up again when you close the small player, unless it was already paused). Closing the small player, or leaving it for another app, stops the file. The progress bar is the same wavy one as the player's. **Open in BoomListener** sends that song to the app's player, continuing from the same point (files from other apps are copied into the app's cache first, so they keep working after the small player closes), and the next file you open slides up over your file manager again, not inside the app. It works even if the song isn't in your library (for example, a file in a folder hidden by your library settings).
- **Keep the screen on** (Settings → Playback → Keep the screen on in): tick where the screen should stay awake while the app is open: the full-screen player (cover and lyrics), only the lyrics, the Home, Library or Profile tab, or the whole app. A switch below (on by default) keeps it awake only while music is playing, so a paused app lets the screen turn off. Nothing is on by default.

### Changed
- **Profile reorganized:** the orange "This month" card now has a "See all" button showing that it opens your Statistics, with the month under "This month", and under your numbers it rotates (every few seconds, or swipe it) through the highlights of the month: your top artist, top song, top genre and record day. Below it come a listening calendar (one square per day for the last 17 weeks, stronger the more you listened, with your longest streak; tap a day, or drag your finger across the squares, to see its date and listening time), your History and the artists you follow. Most played, Listening time and Your artists left the Profile, since the Statistics page already shows them. In December the "Your Wrapped is ready" card got a new look with a "See now" button.
- **Settings icon colors:** the icons in Settings now have their own colors, grouped by category (Appearance purple, Playback blue, Library green, Sharing orange, System teal, About pink), with different colors where the subject differs (bug reports red, privacy green).
- **Wrapped backgrounds have shapes:** instead of a flat color, each Wrapped card now has abstract shapes behind it (a different composition for every card: discs, rings, arcs, blobs, stripes, dots, diamonds, rays, waves and triangles, in the card's own tones, also in the shared image), for depth and personality.
- **Wrapped artists card is now a podium:** your top artist stands big in the middle, the 2nd and 3rd on steps beside it, and the 4th and 5th get their own rows with their little photos.
- **Softer status bar:** a light dark fade now sits under the status bar, so the clock and icons stay readable when covers or titles scroll behind them.
- **History:** the day label stuck at the top now moves aside so the back button no longer covers it.
- **Scroll handle:** in playlists, albums and the history, the fast-scroll handle no longer climbs over the cover and title; it starts where the first song starts.
- **Wrapped sound button:** it now follows the fade-on-pause/play setting like the other players, fading the music out and in instead of cutting it.
- **Notification bells show the ringing bell when on:** the release-alert bell (Releases and artist pages) now has the little bars on both sides when alerts are on.
- **Release date inside the release page:** the full release date (not only the year) is shown under the title of a release.
- **The mini player now "sticks" near its resting place:** if you drag it up and then slowly bring it back down, a small extra push down no longer closes the music; you have to keep pulling past the magnet for it to close.
- **Favoriting pulses the heart:** tapping the heart on the player now makes it swell in place twice, like a heartbeat ("tum tum").
- **Song details open as a bottom panel:** like the other panels, it slides up, dims what is behind, and closes by swiping down or tapping outside (no Close button anymore).
- **Backup is now a collapsed row in Settings → System:** tap it to open "Save backup" and "Restore backup" right there, instead of opening another page.
- **The app now starts with its own colors and the dark theme:** "Wallpaper colors" is off by default (the app keeps its own look) and the default theme is Dark. Anything you already chose in Settings stays as it is. On Android 11 and older the "Wallpaper colors" option no longer appears, since it only works on Android 12 and newer.
- **The quick "Open with" player now fades too:** pausing and playing in the small player that opens for audio files from other apps ramps the volume down and up, like the main player, and follows the same switch in Settings → Playback (off there, off here).
- **Settings lists start closed:** "Keep the screen on in" (Settings → Playback) and the favorite sharing app (Settings → Sharing) are now a single closed row that shows what is chosen; tap it to open the list and pick. It closes again when you leave the screen. In Sharing, each app now shows its icon next to its name.
- **Update notices:** the "Update notices" choice (Settings → System → Updates) now opens as a bottom panel instead of a centered window, like the other pickers.
- **Minimum length label:** from 60 seconds up, the slider now shows minutes and seconds (for example "1:05 min") instead of a long number of seconds.
- **The app is now simply called BoomListener**, the same name you see under the icon, everywhere (About, backups, the share card, the README and the legal pages). It used to be written "Boom, listener!".

### Fixed
- **Dates follow your language:** the dates in the Profile's listening calendar and highlights now use the order and words of your phone's language (for example "19 Nov" or "Nov 19") instead of one fixed format.
- **Clear history is clearer:** the confirmation now says that Most played, your Statistics and your Wrapped keep their own daily summary and aren't affected. The privacy policy says the same, and how to erase that summary (clearing the app's data).
- **Open with, safer:** BoomListener no longer opens a `file://` address that points inside its own private data, if another app sends one.
- **Sharing images:** creating a Wrapped image no longer deletes the song banner you just shared (and the other way around), which could break the share if the other app was still reading it.
- **Leaving a screen stops loading its artist pictures right away,** instead of finishing the lookups first.
- **Wrapped song and other audio:** the song that plays inside the Wrapped now respects the rest of your phone: it pauses during a call or when another app starts playing (and comes back), and it pauses if you unplug your headphones instead of continuing on the speaker.
- **Listening summary safety:** if the phone can't save your daily listening summary at that moment (for example, storage full), it now keeps trying instead of dropping the changes, and the one-time import of your old history is only marked as done once everything was saved. If its small settings file is ever lost, the app no longer imports your old history a second time on top of what is already there.
- **Smoother scrolling:** the fast-scroll bar no longer does extra work on every frame while it is hidden, and the Wrapped stops redrawing while you hold a card to pause it.
- **Search bars and focus:** tapping anywhere outside a search bar (a tap, not a scroll or swipe) now takes the focus away from it and closes the keyboard, in every search of the app. The "x" button only erases what you typed: the keyboard and the focus stay as they are. This also works for the search inside panels that slide up from the bottom (like adding songs to a playlist).
- **Search bars keep blinking after the keyboard closes:** when the keyboard is closed (back button or gesture), the search field now loses focus instead of staying with a blinking cursor, in every search bar of the app.
- **Scroll bar drag:** touching or dragging on the scroll bar's track away from the handle no longer makes it jump there; it only moves when you hold the handle itself (and grabbing it doesn't make it jump either). Elsewhere the list simply scrolls. The letter bubble no longer stays on screen after you let go.
- **Index bubble for symbols:** a song whose title starts with a symbol (like "#doyalike") now shows "#" in the scroll bar bubble instead of the next letter, matching where it sits in the list.
- **Listening stats files:** if a month of your daily listening summary can't be read, the file is kept next to it (with a .corrupt ending) instead of being overwritten by the next listen.
- **Listening calendar drag:** dragging your finger across the squares now follows the finger over several days instead of stopping after the first one.
- **Quick taps while a screen is opening:** tapping Settings and quickly an option inside no longer ignores the second tap; only a real double tap (under 0.2 s) is ignored now, so a screen still can't open twice.
- **Profile cover corners:** no more light gray slivers at the corners of a dark cover image.
- **Mini player progress bar:** it now glides smoothly like the one in the full player instead of moving in small steps.
- **Profile with a solid dark color in the light theme:** titles, names and icons no longer look faded (dark on black); they now follow the profile's own light text color.
- **Full-screen player colors:** with "buttons in the cover's color" on, the audio visualizer and the time bar now use the cover's color too, instead of the system color that clashed with the green/other cover tint.
- **Songs tab header:** touching or sliding your finger over the title area of the search header while it is stuck at the top no longer taps or scrolls whatever is behind it in the list (when it is not stuck, sliding over it scrolls the list as usual).
- **Wrapped cards:** the Wrapped no longer jumps back to the first card if the song card becomes available while it is open.
- **Wrapped:** holding your finger to pause and releasing no longer counts as a tap that jumps to the next or previous card; the bars only fade once the hold is recognized.
- **Artist listed as similar to himself:** in an artist's About tab, a duplicate profile with the same name (no photo, and tapping it did nothing) no longer shows up under "Similar artists".
- **Similar artists names:** in an artist's About tab, long names no longer break in the middle of a word onto a second line (they end with "…"), so "Artist info: Deezer" below no longer jumps around.
- **Search bar in Artists while selecting:** with artists marked, the search bar now stays in its place in the list (like on Home) instead of sticking at the top under the selection bar.
- **Backup panel corners:** the end of the opened Backup list is rounded like the other lists.
- **Artist circles that stayed empty:** when Deezer answered empty or failed while many pictures loaded at once, the circle kept the generic person icon until you left the screen. Now it asks again a couple of seconds later (twice at most), and finding the newest cover takes one request instead of three.
- **Pictures flashing the initial letter or the cover while you scroll fast or jump back to the top:** covers and artist photos are now kept in memory (and the ones at the top of each list are prepared ahead), in Library (Genres, Artists, Playlists, Albums), Home and Profile. Scrolling the rows of Profile by hand no longer makes some artists lose their picture either.
- **Artist photos at the top of the list showing the initial while you tap the tab to go back up:** the photos of the first artists (about a screen and a half in the grid, two in the list) are now loaded ahead and kept in memory, so they are already there when the list slides back to the top.
- **Artist photos in the big grid reloading when you jump back to the top:** the app kept only a few big photos in memory, so after scrolling far and tapping the tab to go back up, they all had to be loaded again (and showed the song cover for a moment). It now keeps many more, in proportion to the memory your phone gives the app, and searches already saved on the phone no longer wait in line behind the ones that need the internet.
- **Artist photos disappearing while scrolling fast:** when the photo search came back empty (it looks like the service limits requests that arrive in a burst), that empty answer was kept for a day, so the photo stayed missing. Empty searches are no longer kept and are asked again the next time, and the app now runs only a few artist searches at once.
- **Tapping the tab to go back to the top stopping working:** if you touched the list while it was scrolling up after tapping the active tab, that tab could stop answering the tap until you switched tabs and came back. It now keeps working (this applies to Home, Library, Releases and Profile).
- **Delete and save confirmations:** the app now closes the keyboard and waits for its own window to have focus before opening the system's "allow BoomListener to delete this file?" window, to avoid the keyboard showing up behind it. The keyboard popping up there was also seen in another app and went away after restarting the phone, so it was most likely an Android glitch rather than something in BoomListener; this change is a precaution.
- **Song going silent after saving its tags:** if you edited the tags of the song that was playing and then dragged the bar to another spot, it could go silent a few seconds later (pausing and playing again brought it back). The song is now reloaded from the same point when you save, so it plays the updated file; you may hear a very short cut at that moment.
- **New songs not showing up:** songs downloaded while the app was in the background could be missing from the library until you restarted it. The library now also reloads every time you come back to the app.
- **Songs that don't say they're music:** audio files that Android doesn't flag as music (for example ones saved straight in your phone's storage by a download app) now show up in the library. Ringtones, notifications and alarms are still left out. If voice messages or other audio show up that you don't want, use **Settings → Library**: minimum duration or only the folders you choose.

### Known issues
- **Tag scan:** it is still in development. "No lyrics" only looks at lyrics saved inside the file, not the ones the app fetched from LRCLIB. On a big library the scan can take a while, and the result is not saved between visits.
- **Selecting songs:** it only works in the Songs list on Home and in album, genre, playlist and favorites pages (not yet in artist pages, search results, history or profile).
- **Profile:** tapping the name or bio, and the previews in the edit sheet, still open the editor or the picture picker.
